02 · INDUSTRIAL CLUSTER ANALYSIS
China Supply Chain Landscape
China produces >70% of global LED street light fixtures, concentrated in three industrial belts with vertically integrated driver, chip, and housing capabilities.
Pearl River Delta
Guangdong (Guangzhou, Foshan, Shenzhen). Core for LED fixtures — high OEM/ODM capacity, strong CE/IEC certification base, direct air freight via Guangzhou Baiyun. Dominant for 100–200W street light segment.
Yangtze River Delta
Jiangsu (Yangzhou, Nanjing). Strong in smart control modules and IoT-integrated systems. Proximity to Shanghai port ensures fast LCL/FCL access. Preferred for NB-IoT dimming controllers.
Egypt Local Suppliers
Egyptian Electrical Industries (10th of Ramadan City) and Delta Light Egypt (Cairo) offer locally assembled LED fixtures meeting ES 4698. Shorter lead time, no import duty, preferred for government tender compliance.
COMPLIANCE & CERTIFICATION PATHWAY
All suppliers must provide valid CE Declaration of Conformity, IEC 60598-2-3 test reports, and ES 4698 equivalence letter from NTRA-accredited lab. For GAPC tender submission, Arabic-language DoC + local importer registration (Commercial Registry No.) required.
